# Educational Video Transcript — English

Between classes and assignments, an urge can feel as if it must be followed
now. But urgency is not a command.

Pause. Breathe in for four counts, hold for two, and breathe out for six. A
short pause creates room to choose an action aligned with your goal.

Name today's goal. Close the access, switch to a safe activity, or contact
someone you trust. You do not have to face the urge alone.

Gamblock-AI is designed as a student support tool: local detection, a Pattern
Interrupt, consent-based accountability, and web self-regulation.

On supported browsers, URL rules and bounded title, heading, and anchor text
are processed with Bag-of-Words and Logistic Regression. The decision stays on
the device.

URLs, domains, DOM content, history, screenshots, and page scores are not sent
to the server. Account and voluntary recovery features have separate purposes
and controls.

When an indication is detected, the native app blocks and shows a seven-second
visual pause. The user can then choose grounding or web recovery without
sending the page address.

An accountability partner is a parent or peer selected through consent. They
can help review removal requests, but cannot see history, journals, mood, or
private recovery detail.

The web journey supports intentions, check-ins, impulse education, small
missions, cross-program skills, and reflection on the next adjustment.

Gamblock-AI is currently a code-complete prototype. Model, device, resilience,
retention, and psychological-outcome evaluation still requires execution and
review.

Start with one pause and one safer choice. Open Gamblock-AI to learn, arrange
support, or find help.
