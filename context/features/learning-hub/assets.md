# Learning Hub Asset Brief

Status: `planned`

## Required visual inventory

- five cluster header illustrations;
- ten mini-project thumbnails;
- optional five career-path illustrations;
- empty, loading, and expired-resource states;
- existing Gami/Recovery Room assets reused where possible.

## Constraints

- Do not use provider logos unless their licensing is explicitly documented.
- Prefer abstract student-life, learning, planning, and project imagery.
- Do not depict gambling, financial loss, diagnosis, or clinical treatment.
- Provide Indonesian and English alt text.
- Provide desktop and mobile-safe crops.
- Respect reduced motion; images must not be required to understand content.

Image generation may be used for original bitmap illustrations after this
brief is approved. Generated images are supporting assets and must not delay
the backend contract, content seed, or accessible text-first UI.
